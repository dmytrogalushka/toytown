TOY TOWN

a 32 cm toy truck in a full-size street

Best played sideways: turn your phone.

Full screen on iPhone: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and open Toy Town from there (Safari doesn't let a page go full screen).

Touchthe stick (bottom left, where your thumb lands): steer, push up for gas, down to brake (on foot: walk) · drag the view to look around · the buttons in the corner change with what you do · II pauses · swipe to pick a character
KeyboardWASD / arrows drive or walk · Space handbrake / jump · Shift run · E get out / take any car · R unstuck · Esc pause menu
Mousemove to look around (it swings back after a moment); click the menus
Xbox controllerRT / LT gas and brake · left stick steer or walk · A handbrake / jump · Y get in / out · B unstuck · right stick look · Menu pause

Made with Rive (scripts, shaders)
